About

Who We Are

Tungava Constructions is a rapidly growing infrastructure company headquartered in Hyderabad, India, working across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

We specialise in control, open and chemical blasting, earthwork excavation, road works and canal works — delivering reliable engineering solutions for public and private-sector clients.

To build enduring infrastructure through innovation, expertise, and a commitment to excellence in every project.

Capabilities

Our Services

Blasting, earthworks, roads and canals under one roof — fewer interfaces, faster execution.

01 / Blasting

Control Blasting

Precision-controlled blasting with minimal vibration for sensitive environments — sites close to existing structures, pipelines or utilities.

02 / Blasting

Open Blasting

Large-scale, high-volume blasting for mining, quarrying and major excavation projects under expert supervision.

03 / Blasting

Chemical Blasting

Non-explosive rock breaking using controlled chemical expansion, for restricted zones where conventional blasting isn't feasible.

04 / Civil

Earthwork Excavations

End-to-end earthmoving for foundations, tunnels and major civil construction — mobilised quickly at scale.

05 / Civil

Road Works

Highways, expressways and urban roads — construction and maintenance to national-standard specifications.

06 / Civil

Canal Works

Canal construction, concrete lining and renovation for irrigation and water-management programmes.

Core Expertise

Blasting Services

Three modes.

We match the blasting technique to the environment — urban, industrial or remote — so every project moves faster without compromising safety or the structural integrity of anything nearby.

Control Blasting

Precision detonation with controlled vibration. Ideal for sites close to existing structures, pipelines or utilities.

Open Blasting

High-volume rock excavation for quarrying, mining and heavy civil works under expert supervision.

Chemical Blasting

Non-explosive expansive chemistry for restricted zones where conventional blasting isn't feasible.
